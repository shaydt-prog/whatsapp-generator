Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how whatsappqr.com (“we”, “us”, the “Service”) handles information when you use our WhatsApp link and QR code generator. We keep it deliberately simple because the tool is designed to collect as little as possible.

1. Phone numbers and messages you enter

The phone number and optional message you type are used only inside your own browser to build a WhatsApp link and QR code. This information is not sent to or stored on our servers. When you close the tab, it is gone.

2. Information we collect automatically

Like most websites, we use analytics services (Google Analytics and Microsoft Advertising / Bing) to understand aggregate traffic — for example how many people visit and which pages are popular. These services may set cookies and collect standard technical data such as your approximate location, device type and pages viewed. We do not use this data to identify you personally.

3. Payments

If you choose the optional Pro upgrade, payment is processed by PayPal. We never see or store your full card details. PayPal receives the information necessary to complete your purchase and handles it under its own privacy policy. We store only a record on your own device (in your browser's local storage) that the upgrade was purchased, so Pro features stay unlocked for you.

4. Cookies

Cookies are used for analytics and advertising measurement as described above. You can disable cookies in your browser settings; the core generator will still work.

5. Third parties

We rely on a small number of third-party services: Google Analytics, Microsoft Advertising, and PayPal. Each processes data under its own policies. We do not sell your personal information.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. Because we do not maintain personal accounts or store the numbers you enter, most requests relate to analytics data held by the third parties above. Contact us and we will help where we can.

7. Children

This Service is intended for general business and personal use and is not directed at children under 13.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above reflects the current version.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: support@whatsappqr.com.
